Private by design

It makes no network requests.
Not one.

Every name is worked out from the page already open in front of you. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking in this extension.

Your analytics stay yours

No traffic figures, revenue, user counts, property names or account numbers are collected, transmitted or stored off your device. Ever.

Works entirely offline

Names come from the Google Analytics page itself and, if you allow it, from the extensions already installed in your own Chrome profile.

Nothing in Analytics is altered

The replacement is display-only and lives in your browser. Your Google Analytics configuration, data and reports are untouched.

Open source, no build step

Plain JavaScript with no dependencies and no bundler. What ships is what you can read on GitHub.

Permissions requested
storagesaves the names you choose
analytics.google.comthe only site it runs on

No remote code, no third-party libraries, no analytics on the analytics tool.
